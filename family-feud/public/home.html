How it works

  1. Start a game and type in your questions. Or just play the sample game.
  2. Open the TV page on the computer that's plugged into the TV, and press F11 for full screen.
  3. Send each team captain their link (or the 4-letter code). They open it on their phone — no app or account needed.
  4. Run the game from your phone or laptop. Open buzzers, tap answers to flip them, give strikes, and watch the scores.
